Privacy Policy

How LogicHub - F.Z.E collects, manages, and protects business information and personal data under UAE statutory regulations.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Corporate Entity & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to LogicHub - F.Z.E, a Free Zone Establishment with Limited Liability registered in Ajman Free Zone, UAE (License No. 57090). This document governs data collection across our digital touchpoints and corporate inquiries.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect business and contact information submitted directly by you when requesting consultations or proposals, including:

  • Name and company entity details
  • Official corporate email address and telephone numbers
  • Project briefs, scope requirements, and event logistics data

3. Purpose of Processing

Information collected is used strictly to provide service consultations, prepare quotations, fulfill project deliverables across our ten licensed business activities, and maintain business communication. We do not monetize or sell corporate information.

4. UAE Regulatory Compliance

LogicHub complies with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 regarding the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL). Appropriate administrative, technical, and operational safeguards are maintained to protect data integrity and prevent unauthorized access.

5. Contact Regarding Privacy

For inquiries regarding personal data records or compliance, please write to: info@logichub.ae or contact our Ajman Free Zone desk.
